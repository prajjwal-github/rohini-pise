Dr. Rohini Pise is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Information Technology at Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering, Nigdi, Pune. She has been part of the institute since 2005 — as a Lecturer through 2012, and as Assistant Professor since June 2012 — and completed her Ph.D. in Computer Engineering at PCCoE, SPPU, in 2024.

Her teaching spans the security stack end to end: from the physical and link layers in Data Communication and Computer Networks, through Cryptography and Network Security, to the applied frontier in Blockchain Technology, smart contracts and Hyperledger Fabric. Course notes, lab assignments and question banks for every subject are published openly for her students.

Beyond the classroom she is a member of IEEE and LMISTE, has served as reviewer and session chair across ICCUBEA, cPGCON, iPGCon and NGCT, and holds a published patent and a registered copyright in assistive voice technology for visually impaired users.
